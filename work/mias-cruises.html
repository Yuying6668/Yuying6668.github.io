AI-powered Voice of Customer platform for Mia's Cruises

Customer feedback lives everywhere: reviews, surveys, internal reports, in several languages. I built a GenAI platform that brings it together with internal business data, so a team can ask a question in plain language and get an answer backed by citations, not guesswork.

01The problem

Customer feedback was fragmented across sources and languages. That made consistent reporting and root-cause analysis slow, manual and subjective: two analysts could read the same reviews and reach different conclusions.

Generic chatbots don't fix this. They answer confidently even when they're wrong, and nobody can check where the answer came from. For a business decision, an answer you can't verify is worse than no answer.

02The approach

I designed the platform around one principle: every insight must be traceable to evidence.

1 · IngestStandardise multilingual feedback batches
2 · RetrieveHybrid search: keyword + semantic
3 · ReasonLangGraph workflow: route → retrieve → answer
4 · CiteAnswers with citations, or abstain
5 · ActDashboard for insights, drafts & approvals
  • Ingestion pipeline that cleans and standardises feedback from different sources and languages into versioned batches.
  • Hybrid retrieval combines keyword and semantic search, so exact terms (a ship name, a route) and fuzzy meaning ("felt rushed at boarding") both get found.
  • Three-agent LangGraph workflow: one agent routes the question, one retrieves evidence, one answers with citations. The model is allowed to say "I don't know".
  • React dashboard where teams explore insights, review AI-drafted summaries and approve them before they're shared.

03Measuring it

I treated the system like a product, not a demo. It was evaluated on a frozen test set, with traces in Langfuse:

92%task accuracy
86%Top-5 retrieval recall
500+test queries evaluated

Beyond accuracy, the evaluation also tracks citation correctness, abstention accuracy, P95 latency and failure rate, broken down by language, route and source. That way weak spots show up before users find them.

04Trust & governance

Customer data is sensitive, especially under GDPR. Governance is built in from the start, not added at the end:

  • Redacted observability: traces store only hashes, versions, timings, evidence IDs and scores. Raw prompts, reviews and identities never go to monitoring tools.
  • Audit logs record which workflow version and configuration produced each answer.
  • Deletion workflow: removing an upload batch also removes it from embeddings, caches and traces.
  • Fail-closed production mode: production can only be switched on when every governance check (e.g. DPIA, data residency, retention) has evidence.

Note: the demo uses synthetic survey data. It shows how the system works and is not evidence about real passengers.

05Why it matters

This project is where my two worlds meet. My PhD taught me that why people feel something matters more than how often they do something. This platform lets a business read the why in thousands of customer voices, at scale and with evidence.
