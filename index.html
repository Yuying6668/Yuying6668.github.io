About

From understanding people to building data products

My path started in journalism and psychology. I spent my PhD at the University of Salamanca asking a very business-relevant question: which user behaviours actually matter, and which metrics are misleading? Analysing 1,200+ users cross-sectionally and ~2,000 over two years, I found that why people use a platform predicts satisfaction far better than how often they use it.

Since then I've moved that thinking into business: building KPI frameworks and Power BI dashboards, delivering attribution analytics that lifted ROI from 1.30 to 1.45, and developing a GenAI platform that turns messy customer feedback into cited, trustworthy insights.

I'm now based in the Copenhagen area, learning Danish (Module 3.3), and looking for a role where BI foundations and AI capabilities meet real stakeholder needs.

AI-powered Voice of Customer Platform — Mia's Cruises

A GenAI customer intelligence platform that combines external customer feedback with internal business data, so teams can ask questions in plain language and get cited, evidence-based answers.

Problem

Customer feedback was fragmented across sources and languages, which made consistent reporting and root-cause analysis slow and subjective.

What I built

  • Ingestion pipeline that standardises multilingual feedback
  • RAG with hybrid (keyword + semantic) retrieval
  • Three-agent LangGraph workflow: route → retrieve → answer with citations
  • React dashboard for insights, drafts and approvals
  • Governance: redacted traces, audit logs, deletion workflow
